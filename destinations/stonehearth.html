Official Destination Guide

Stonehearth

Heart of the Mountains

Overview

Stonehearth is one of the principal mountain communities of western Elemara.

Its engineering traditions, extensive stone construction, and surrounding alpine landscape make it an important destination for visitors interested in industry, geology, and mountain travel.

Quick Facts

Region Western Highlands
Climate Cool Mountain
Travel Status Open
Nearest USICA Office Grayhaven

Getting There

Stonehearth is reached through established overland transportation routes from Grayhaven. Seasonal weather may affect travel conditions.

Climate

Visitors should prepare for cooler temperatures and rapidly changing mountain weather.

Local Customs

Many public buildings remain active places of work. Visitors are encouraged to remain within designated public areas unless accompanied by authorized guides.
