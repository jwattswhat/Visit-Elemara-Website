About Elemara

A World Beyond Earth

Welcome

Elemara is a distant blue world of mountains, marshlands, forests, inland seas, and ancient cities. Humans, dwarves, elves, trolls, and goblins have shaped its history through cultures and traditions extending far into the past.

Much of Elemara remains unknown. The information presented here is limited to places, peoples, and traditions that can be introduced without revealing the events of The Blue Planet.

A World Worth Exploring

From quiet marshlands and towering mountain ranges to fertile valleys and protected wilderness, every region of Elemara offers its own landscapes, traditions, and history.

USICA encourages all visitors to respect local customs, protected environments, and current travel advisories.
