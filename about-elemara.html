About Elemara

Humanity's Newest World

Welcome

Elemara is humanity's first permanent extrasolar colony. Located approximately one hundred light-years from Earth, the planet is home to a growing population of settlers, researchers, educators, and public servants.

Although exploration continues across much of the world, several established communities now welcome official visitors through approved transportation corridors.

A World Worth Exploring

From quiet marshlands and towering mountain ranges to fertile valleys and protected wilderness, every region of Elemara offers its own landscapes, traditions, and history.

USICA encourages all visitors to respect local customs, protected environments, and current travel advisories.
