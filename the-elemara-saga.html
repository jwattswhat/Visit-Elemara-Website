The Elemara Saga

Every world has a story.

Visit Elemara was created as a companion experience for The Elemara Saga, a science fiction and fantasy series by Jonathan C. Watt.

While the website presents the world through the perspective of the United States Interstellar Colonization Authority, the novels tell the stories of the people who live there.

About the Author

Jonathan C. Watt is a Lutheran pastor, writer, and lifelong student of history, language, and theology.

Through The Elemara Saga, he combines science fiction and fantasy to explore faith, culture, sacrifice, and what it means to be human.
