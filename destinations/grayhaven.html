Official Destination Guide

Grayhaven

Humanity's First Settlement

Overview

Grayhaven serves as the principal administrative center of humanity's permanent settlement on Elemara.

Most official visitors begin their journey here before continuing to destinations throughout the planet.

Quick Facts

Founded 2184
Status Colonial Capital
Population Growing
Travel Status Open

Government Services

Grayhaven is home to the principal administrative offices of the United States Interstellar Colonization Authority, including Visitor Information, Public Safety, and Transportation.
