The Elemara Saga

Every world has a story.

Visit Elemara was created as a companion experience for The Elemara Saga, a science fiction and fantasy series by Jonathan C. Watt.

The website uses the visual language of the United States Interstellar Colonization Authority while withholding the novel's discoveries, outcomes, and central mysteries.

The Blue Planet

When a human voyage goes catastrophically off course, one traveler finds himself stranded on a distant world inhabited by ancient peoples. Unable to speak their languages or understand their conflicts, he must learn whom to trust—and discover why he was brought to Elemara.

A story of discovery, sacrifice, faith, and the meeting of civilizations.

About the Author

Jonathan C. Watt is a Lutheran pastor, writer, and lifelong student of history, language, and theology.

Through The Elemara Saga, he combines science fiction and fantasy to explore faith, culture, sacrifice, and what it means to be human.
