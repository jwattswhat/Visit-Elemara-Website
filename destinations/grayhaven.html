Official Destination Guide

Grayhaven

Gateway to the Interior

Overview

Grayhaven is a harbor city on Elemara's northern coast and a natural gateway to routes leading inland.

Its docks, markets, and busy streets make it a natural starting point for exploring the wider world.

Quick Facts

Region Northern Coast
Status Harbor City
Population Growing
Travel Status Open

Harbor and Markets

Grayhaven's working harbor connects the coast with inland roads. Markets near the waterfront offer goods, food, and news from across Elemara.
