Official Destination Guide

Dragon Island

Protected Natural Reserve

Overview

Dragon Island is one of Elemara's most carefully managed protected regions.

The island supports a wide variety of native wildlife and contains several environmentally sensitive habitats. Visitor access is regulated in cooperation with local authorities.

Quick Facts

Region Eastern Inland Sea
Climate Temperate Maritime
Travel Status Restricted
Permit Required Yes

Getting There

Access is available only through approved transportation providers and authorized visitor programs. Independent travel is not permitted.

Climate

Weather conditions may change rapidly. Visitors should be prepared for wind, rain, and variable temperatures throughout the year.

Visitor Conduct

Visitors are expected to follow all instructions provided by local guides and preserve protected sites for future generations.
