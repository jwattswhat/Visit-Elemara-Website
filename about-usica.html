About USICA

United States Interstellar Colonization Authority

The United States Interstellar Colonization Authority provides public information for the settlement, exploration, and long-term development of humanity's first permanent extrasolar colony.

Our Mission

USICA supports safe travel, scientific research, public education, and responsible stewardship of the inhabited regions of Elemara.

Public Information

VisitElemara.com provides official maps, destination guides, publications, and historical reference materials prepared for residents, researchers, educators, and visitors.

Working Together

USICA works in cooperation with local governments, communities, and cultural organizations throughout Elemara to encourage mutual understanding and responsible exploration.
